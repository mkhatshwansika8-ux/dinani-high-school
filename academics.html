Our curriculum follows Eswatini's national secondary school framework, guiding learners from Form 1 through to Form 5 examinations.

Curriculum

Curriculum Information

Dinani High School follows the standard Eswatini secondary curriculum. Learners in the lower forms work towards the Junior Certificate (JC), while senior learners in Form 5 sit the Eswatini General Certificate of Secondary Education (EGCSE) examinations.
