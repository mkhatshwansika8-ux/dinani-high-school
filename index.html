Dinani High School is a secondary school in Buhleni, Eswatini, committed to mentoring young minds through strong academics, discipline and a supportive school community. We prepare every learner for success in the classroom and in life.

Welcome to Dinani High School

A Message From Our Principal

On behalf of the staff, students and parents of Dinani High School, welcome. Our school is more than a place of learning — it is a community where every learner is known, encouraged and challenged to reach their full potential.

We believe that education goes beyond the classroom. At Dinani High School, we nurture academic excellence alongside strong character, discipline and respect, preparing our students not only for examinations, but for life beyond school. We invite you to explore what our school has to offer, and to become part of the Dinani family.

Mr N. Pillay School Principal
