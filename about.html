Our Story

School History

Dinani High School was established in 2013 in Buhleni, Eswatini, with a commitment to mentoring young minds and building a school community rooted in knowledge, character and excellence. Since then, the school has grown into a recognised centre of secondary education in the area.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of academic and moral excellence in Eswatini, nurturing learners who are knowledgeable, disciplined and ready to contribute meaningfully to their communities and nation.

Our Mission

To provide quality, values-based education that develops the full potential of every learner — academically, socially and morally — within a safe, supportive and well-disciplined school environment.

On Our Campus

School Facilities

Classrooms

Dedicated teaching spaces for each form level, supporting focused, structured learning.

Library

A resource centre supporting research, private study and a love of reading among students.

Science Laboratory

Practical learning space for Biology, Chemistry and Physics coursework and experiments.

Computer Laboratory

Access to computer studies and digital literacy training for students.

Sports Grounds

Outdoor space for athletics, football, netball and inter-house sporting events.

Assembly Hall

A shared space for school assemblies, exams, cultural events and ceremonies.
